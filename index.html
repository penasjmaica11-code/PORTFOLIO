My Journey at Cebu Eastern College

As a fourth-year student in the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program at Cebu Eastern College, I discovered that my favorite intersection lives right where logic meets visual aesthetics. While learning algorithms, databases, and software principles, I naturally gravitated toward front-end design and browser styling.

I love the feeling of translating static ideas, wireframes, and mood boards into interactive, accessible, and responsive code. Every class project has pushed me to improve my design hierarchy, responsive grid skills, and eye for joyful color palettes.
